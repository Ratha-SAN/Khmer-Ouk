1. Who we are

Ouk Chatrang (oukkhmer.com, also at ouk.bykhmer.com) is a free web game of traditional Cambodian chess, run by an independent developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game collects, why, who sees it, and the choices you have. You can contact us at {{CONTACT}}.

2. The short version

  • You can play as a guest without giving us your name or email.
  • If you sign in, we store the details needed to run your account and your game statistics.
  • We use Google Firebase for accounts, data storage and usage statistics, and Cloudflare to host the site.
  • We do not sell your personal information. We do not show advertising at the moment; if we do in future it will be provided by Google AdSense, with your consent where the law requires it (section 6).
  • You can ask us to show, correct or delete your data at any time.

4. How we use information

  • To run the game: accounts, sign-in, profiles, statistics, saved games and online matches.
  • To understand how the game is used, to fix errors and to improve it.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.
  • To answer your messages and requests.
  • To meet legal obligations.

Where the law of your country requires a legal basis (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), we rely on: performing our agreement with you (accounts and game features); our legitimate interests (security, understanding use, improving the game); and your consent where the law requires it.

5. Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers that run the game, and with other players as described above.

  • Google (Firebase and Google Analytics) provides accounts (Authentication), our database (Firestore) and usage statistics. See policies.google.com/privacy and firebase.google.com/support/privacy.
  • Cloudflare hosts and delivers the site and sees your IP address and request details when you load it. See cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.
  • jsDelivr delivers some open-source code libraries the game uses, so it sees your IP address and browser details when they load. See jsdelivr.com.
  • Google, Facebook and Apple, only if you choose to sign in with them. They receive the sign-in request under their own policies.
  • Other players see your display name (and avatar) in online games and open invitations. Choose a display name you are comfortable sharing.
  • Authorities and advisers, when the law requires it or to protect our rights, and a successor if the game is transferred to someone else.

6. Advertising

At the time this policy was last updated, the game does not show advertising. We are preparing to show some on certain pages, such as the guides and the end-of-game screen. When we switch advertising on, it will be provided by Google AdSense, a third-party advertising service, and this section describes what will then apply.

  • What ad providers may collect. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies and similar technologies, and information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you view, to show and measure ads, to limit how often you see an ad, and to detect fraud and abuse. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.
  • Your consent. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), we will ask for your consent through a consent tool before ads that use cookies for personalisation are shown, and you will be able to change your choice later.
  • Your controls. You can manage personalised advertising from Google at adssettings.google.com and find opt-out tools for many advertising companies at aboutads.info. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser; the game keeps working.
  • What we do not do. We do not sell your personal information. We keep ads away from game controls, and we do not use ads to collect information from you beyond what this policy describes. An advertiser does not receive your name or email address from us.

Advertisers and ad providers are third parties with their own privacy policies, and we do not control them. Before advertising is switched on we will make sure this section is up to date.

7. International transfers

Our providers run services worldwide, so your information may be processed in countries other than your own, including the United States. They describe the safeguards they use in their own policies, linked above.

8. How long we keep information

  • Account, profile and game records are kept until you ask us to delete them, or until we remove accounts or records that are no longer needed.
  • Visit records are not linked to you and are kept for as long as they are useful for understanding how the game is used.
  • Usage statistics are kept by Google Analytics for the retention period set in our analytics account.
  • Information on your device stays until you clear it.

9. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show you the personal information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, give you a copy, or stop using it for a purpose. You can also withdraw consent you have given. To do any of this, email {{CONTACT}} from the address on your account (or tell us how to identify your account) and we will respond within a reasonable time, normally within 30 days.

  • In the game: you can change your display name and avatar in your profile, sign out at any time, and clear information stored on your device in your browser settings.
  • Guests: a guest account is tied to an anonymous ID stored in your browser, so we cannot tell which records are yours unless you can give us that ID. Clearing your browser's site data removes the link on your device.
  • Usage statistics: you can limit these with your browser's privacy settings or a content blocker. The game keeps working.
  • If you are in the EEA, UK or similar region: you may also object to or restrict processing, and you have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority.
  • If you are in California or another US state with a privacy law: you have rights to know, delete and correct. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

10. Children

The game is for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from a child under 13. If you are under the age at which you can consent to data processing in your country (13 to 16, depending on where you live), please play as a guest or ask a parent or guardian before creating an account. If you believe a child has given us personal information, email {{CONTACT}} and we will delete it.

11. Security

Connections to the game use HTTPS. Our database rules mean that you can read and change only your own profile and game records. No online service is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Keep your sign-in details private.

12. Links and third-party sign-in

The game may link to other sites. They have their own policies and we are not responsible for them.

We may update this policy. We will change the date at the top, and for significant changes we will also tell players in the game (for example in What's New). Using the game after a change means you accept the updated policy.
