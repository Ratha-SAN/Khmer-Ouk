Ouk Chatrang is a free web game of traditional Cambodian chess, playable in your browser at oukkhmer.com, on a phone or a computer, in Khmer and English. It is made and maintained by an independent developer.

▸ លេងឥឡូវ▸ Play nowវិធីលេងHow to play

អ្វីដែលអ្នកអាចធ្វើបានWhat you can do

  • Play the computer at six levels, from a starter level for a very first game up to expert, and move up a level after each win.
  • Play with a friend on one device, or online by sending an invitation link.
  • Learn to play with lessons for every piece on a real board, and with the How to play guide.
  • Solve the daily puzzles: three checkmate and tactics problems a day, at three difficulty levels.
  • Choose how it looks. There are 3D and flat 2D board themes, background scenes, lighting controls, sound effects and music.
  • Play offline. After your first visit the game keeps working without a connection, and you can install it on your home screen like an app. Online play and sign-in need the internet.

ឥតគិតថ្លៃ និងគណនីFree play and accounts

You can start playing as a guest straight away. A free account unlocks the stronger computer levels, more themes and saving your progress and statistics. The details of what we store are in the Privacy Policy, and the rules for using the game are in the Terms of Use.

កុំព្យូទ័រលេងយ៉ាងដូចម្តេចHow the computer opponent works

The computer uses the same rules engine that checks every move on the board, so it never makes an illegal move. How strong it is depends on the level:

  • Level 0, the starter, picks its moves at random and takes a piece only now and then. It is there for a very first game: to try moving, capturing and giving checkmate.
  • Level 1 looks one move ahead for each side like the levels above it, but it often settles for a weaker move and now and then makes a careless one. It is about 60% as strong as level 2.
  • Level 2 mixes a short look-ahead search with a choice among several reasonable moves.
  • Level 3 searches two plies ahead (one move by each side), and a little deeper once the other side has almost nothing left, so that it can finish a won game.
  • Levels 4 and 5 search progressively deeper within a time limit of roughly one to two seconds, and level 5 thinks the longest.

It evaluates positions mainly by material, using the piece values you can see in the strategy guide, and it knows the counting rules: it treats a count that runs out as the draw it is, and it avoids shuffling a piece back and forth. It is a hobby engine, not a world-class one, so patient players can beat the top levels.

ហេតុអ្វីWhy this site exists

The aim of this site is simple: to make Ouk Chatrang easy to play and to learn, in Khmer as well as in English, with rules that follow the Cambodian game. Because sources and players differ on some details, the game offers settings for them and says so openly (see the history and counting rules pages).
